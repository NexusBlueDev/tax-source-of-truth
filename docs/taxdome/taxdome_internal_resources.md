# TaxDome Comprehensive Information Resources

This document compiles resources from TaxDome's official materials, covering all aspects of the platform: client-facing features, firm-facing management, workflows, billing, integrations, security, reports, and more. It includes both client and firm perspectives for complete coverage. Structured for easy parsing by LLMs, with clear sections, subsections, and key-value pairs (description and URL).

## Overview
- **Purpose**: Provide a full reference for answering questions about TaxDome, including client portal usage, file sharing, firm setup, workflows, billing, integrations, security, and support. Covers everything from onboarding to advanced features.
- **Sources**: Official TaxDome Help Center (firm: https://help.taxdome.com/; client: https://client-help.taxdome.com/), Academy, and related sites.
- **Last Updated**: Based on information as of January 22, 2026.
